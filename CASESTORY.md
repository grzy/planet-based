# planet based ✺ case story

## 2026-07-19 -- the studio decision

Product-design listings want 1-2 years of experience a masters doesn't substitute for. Instead of waiting, Ivy is opening her own studio under the planet based name and taking on clients to build the experience directly. planetbased.io chosen ("modern and techy") and verified available.

## 2026-07-19 -- killing the bird reveal (the best decision so far)

First hero concept: MJ-generated birds hidden under a masked reveal layer. Ivy killed it for three reasons that became the design's spine:

1. AI renditions of real species, shown next to those species' real recorded songs, misrepresent the thing the studio claims to honor.
2. The style-matching labor would never pay off.
3. **In real life you hear birds you never see.** That truth became the mechanic.

New concept, "the unseen bird": hover a pocket of foliage → the leaves tremble (driven by the recording's live amplitude), the real song plays, and a live waveform thread becomes the only portrait of the bird. Everything on screen is a plant or real data.

## 2026-07-19 -- the unseen bird, v1 shipped

Five birds hide in the wall, all recorded in Aotearoa by real field recordists. Turn the sound on, drift over a pocket of foliage: the song fades in, the leaves tremble in expanding wavefronts driven by the recording's live loudness (the "GPU reads animation data" technique from the week-29 Creative Tech brief -- the radar routine paid for itself), and a thin waveform thread rises out of the greenery. The only portrait of the bird is the shape of its voice, plus a chip crediting the human who stood there with a microphone. A `?place` mode lets Ivy drag the zones herself -- she casts the wall, not the code.

Debugging note for the case story: the wall rendered as smeared streaks for three rounds of "fix the texture" until the actual bug turned out to be one character of operator precedence in the vertex shader's Y-flip. The texture was innocent the whole time.

## 2026-07-19 -- the wall

Prompt iterated twice: v1 produced weird pale moss; v2 zoomed out, cut lichen, and stacked flower diversity (passionflower earned a permanent spot). Ivy generated 7 finals, upscaled in Pixelmator Pro ML (Midjourney's upscale button vanished into their new pipeline). Scaffold shipped with all 7 live-switchable on the page; wall5 leading -- deep mossy center where a song with no visible singer belongs.
