Leaf LitterMatter.js · Canvas · cut paper

Move, and cut-paper leaves, seeds, moths and beetles drop from the cursor, flutter, and pile up on the wordmark.

Grab and throw them; double-click for a gust. Every piece is generated in code, never the same twice.

After Mary Delany's paper mosaicks on black (1772-82). Signature color: vermilion.
Song RollThree.js verlet paper · Web Audio · FFT

A paper roll hangs in swags across the dark, scored in pencil from four real, credited bird recordings.

Pick a voice: it punches the song into the paper in time with the audio. The bits fall, and light comes through the holes onto the wall.

Like a pianola roll, the holes are the song. Move the cursor to stir the paper. Signature color: chartreuse.
Nectar GuidesFragment shader · ping-pong trail

A generative meadow seen from above. To us the flowers look plain.

Where you move, the meadow switches to a pollinator's view: ultraviolet bullseyes and guide lines, seen through a compound eye, fading like a scent trail.

Real biology as the visual language. Signature color: ultraviolet violet.
Local prototypes. Run from the repo root (python3 -m http.server) so the recordings in sounds/ load. Desktop first.
